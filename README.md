1. Business Problem

Small and marginal farmers struggle to sell their crops at fair prices due to multiple middlemen, lack of real-time market information, inefficient transportation, and post-harvest losses. At the same time, urban consumers face fluctuating prices and inconsistent availability of fresh produce, creating a gap between farmers and buyers. 

2. Objectives

Connect farmers directly with buyers.

Predict market demand using AI.

Optimize transportation routes to reduce delivery cost and time.

Provide dynamic price prediction for better farmer profits.

Reduce food wastage through efficient logistics and inventory management.

Improve transparency and efficiency in the agricultural supply chain.

5. Study of the Company Problem Statement

Crop-Connect focuses on solving challenges in agricultural marketing by using AI to connect farmers directly with buyers. The platform predicts market demand, recommends the best buyers, optimizes delivery routes, estimates fair prices, and minimizes food wastage. This improves farmer profits while ensuring consumers receive fresh produce at reasonable prices.

8. Project Scope

Crop-Connect aims to build an AI-powered agricultural marketplace that connects farmers directly with buyers. The system covers farmer and buyer management, crop inventory, AI demand forecasting, dynamic pricing, logistics optimization, warehouse management, order tracking, and analytics. It reduces food wastage, increases farmer income, and creates a transparent, efficient farm-to-market supply chain.
